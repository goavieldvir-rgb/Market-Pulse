Insider buying

Open-market purchases of $10K or more by officers, directors and 10%+ owners (official SEC Form 4 filings), last 30 days, largest first.

Insider selling

Largest open-market sales over the last 30 days. A noisier signal than buying: insiders sell for many reasons. Sales marked 10b5-1 plan were scheduled in advance and say even less.

Sector leaderboard

All 11 SPDR sector ETFs ranked by 5-day return, with the S&P 500 (SPY) as the benchmark row.

Ranks are relative: in a down week the leaders can still be negative. What matters is whether a sector is beating SPY.

52-week highs and lows

Closing price vs the highest high and lowest low of the past 52 weeks. Near highs: momentum and breakout candidates. Near lows: beaten-down names for contrarian research.

Unusual volume

The last session's volume vs its 20-day average, with that day's price move. Heavy volume on a big up day reads very differently from heavy volume on a big down day.

Testing key averages

Stocks sitting close to a moving average: just above it can act as support, just below as resistance. Pick the average and how close counts as near.
