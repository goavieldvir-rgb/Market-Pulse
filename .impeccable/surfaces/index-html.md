# Surface brief: Market Pulse site (dashboard, scanner, weekly recap)

Scope: index.html (Operate: today's market read), opportunity.html (Operate: screens and insider filings), weekly.html (Read: Sunday pre-open recap). One shared world.
Audience/job: public retail traders checking on a phone around the US open; know the market's state in seconds, then the evidence.
Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, no build; every reading shows freshness; "Not financial advice" on every page; must not rely on hue alone for up/down.
Memorable moment: the seven signals plotted as dots on one shared −100…+100 scale under a huge serif composite figure.
Unresolved: none.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The market pages. The site reads like the market-data section of a financial paper turned into a working tool: one headline figure, then honest dense tables. It refuses the neon-glow dark card grid and the semicircle gauge.

OWN-WORLD: Warm newsprint-peach ground (day) and ink-brown night edition chosen by the OS; ink-black text; a sturdy newspaper serif (Source Serif 4) for the masthead, headings and big figures; Barlow / Barlow Semi Condensed with tabular figures for every label and data row; hairline rules, a 3px masthead rule, no shadows, square-ish 2px corners; opportunity in press blue, caution in vermilion, always paired with ▲/▼ or a word; stale data gets a STALE stamp, never hidden.

STORY: The visitor sees today's composite and its zone, sees which signals pull which way on one scale, then reads the evidence rows; the scanner and the weekly recap are other sections of the same paper.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead "Market Pulse" with dateline and market state; section tabs Today / Scanner / Weekly recap; the "Markets at a glance" box: composite figure at ~100px serif, zone word, freshness line, a ruled −100…+100 scale with marker, and the 3-month history line; directly below, "Seven signals, one scale" dot rows.

FORM: The market pages (financial-press market data section), position 3 of the ordered grounded list; seed key 60ed86c6. Raises: scale contrast from the type specimen (one enormous figure, everything else small); the ticket wallet's states that stamp and never disappear (STALE stamps); the botanical folio's fixed shared scale across all plates (every signal on the same −100…+100 axis); the orienteering map's reserved ink (blue/vermilion only ever mean opportunity/caution or up/down, nothing decorative).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
