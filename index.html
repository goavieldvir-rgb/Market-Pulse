Not financial advice. This dashboard summarizes public market data for informational purposes only. Composite scoring is a simplified heuristic, not a signal to buy or sell anything.

Data: Yahoo Finance (price, moving averages, VIX, sectors), CNN Fear & Greed (unofficial public endpoint), AAII.com (weekly sentiment survey), a community-maintained S&P 500 constituent list for breadth. Some figures are best-effort approximations of proprietary index tickers (e.g. the S5FI-style breadth reading); see each entry's note.

Prices refresh about every 10 minutes while the US market is open; breadth updates after each close and AAII weekly. Every entry shows how current its data is.
