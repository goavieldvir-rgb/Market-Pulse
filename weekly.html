Not financial advice. The weekly recap summarizes public market data, news and official trade disclosures for informational purposes only. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell any security.

Prices, sector moves, yields, commodities, bitcoin and the week's insider filings are computed from Yahoo Finance and SEC EDGAR data; the written sections are drafted each Sunday with AI assistance from the sources listed in each edition and reviewed by the editor before publication. Politicians' trades are reported with delays of up to six weeks.

Privacy. No accounts, cookies, ads or analytics. Your watchlist, scanner settings, language and day/night choice are saved only in your own browser. Fonts are served from this site. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visits as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.
